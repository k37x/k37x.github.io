#####Beginning
With the increasing lack of documentation, I figured a nice write up would help those looking to do the same process themselves. Waiting on a tune now as we speak but I wanted to go over my process of purchasing everything, putting it together and so on. I grabbed my 2003 Mustang 3.8 V6 at 70k miles about 2 years ago(August 2020) with about zero knowledge in how cars worked. I wanted to learn and what better way than an old used car. I have made more mistakes than I can count(about 2 weeks in I put the jack through the floor pan, whoops...). About a month ago I decided to embark on supercharging my V6, so lets start out with the parts I bought and what's needed.

#####IAT

- IAT sensor - 9C1Z-12A697-B Ford
- TAP SIZE: 3/8-18 37/64 or 3/8 NPT Tap
- IAT pigtail - 3U2Z-14S411-JZA - Ford

#####IAT2

- IAT2 Sensor 2R3A-9F479-AA
- IAT2 Pigtail 3U2Z14S411SPA
#####IAC

- IAC SENSOR 2R3V9F715AA Ford

NOTE: IAC is on Plenum TB/Elbow, IAT is inside M112 Adapter Plate

IAT/MAF are integrated on V6 so will need to splice harness to attach them*

“I had to lengthen my IAC connector and add some wire to the IAT2 sensor pigtail. The IAT2 sensor gets spliced into the existing IAT sensor pigtail. If you have a 01-04 the wires for the IAT are with the MAF harness.”

####The Install

I did the entire install outside over the course of the last week or so? I didn't have a garage to work in unfortunately but thankfully the weather has been nice where I am. The install went in 3 phases, I did the fuel injectors first, threw everything back on and ran it(smelled awful but it worked), fuel pump , then installing the M112. This spanned over a few days.

https://imgur.com/a/KBaG5JG Here are all the pictures, threw them into an Imgur album as embedding all of these would've been a nightmare.

An angle grinder became my best friend for a lot of the project. Prepare to rip the M112 on and off a lot to make sure everything just right. Any questions I'd be happy to answer. So far the Mustang idles okay but I will be tuning it and following up with that(linking to that post here when I do for future viewers).

Hope this helps anyone trying, if I forgot to mention something I'll edit this post.

####UPDATE:

So finally got a base tune for my setup, had some initial issues that I’d like to share for those that experience anything similar. My first main issue was my idle was extremely high, this was due to my IAC being the wrong direction. Once I flipped it, I had zero issues idle wise! My second main issue was I was experiencing spark knock on acceleration. I was running stock motorcraft plugs gapped at .54. I bought NGK TR6 plugs cause I figured if I have to regap them might as well get new plugs and follow what others had for their setups. Once I gapped them to .34, I had no issues with acceleration. I hope this helps anyone suffering the same issue.
